07 — faq

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Degenpad compiles the instructions from the form above. If you can fill in four fields, you can deploy an SPL token.

Who owns the token after launch?

You do. The full supply is minted directly to your wallet. Degenpad never takes custody, and with mint authority revoked no additional supply can ever be created — not by us, not by anyone.

What does revoking an authority actually do?

Mint authority controls whether new tokens can be printed, freeze authority whether wallets can be frozen, update authority whether metadata can change. Revoking sets each to null on-chain, permanently and verifiably.

Where does the launch fee go?

The flat fee is paid to the Degenpad treasury address shown in the summary card — AEzNumbq6EUNW7P51mijp7LbS4HnLWtoFGcXQMvKija9 — in the same transaction that deploys your mint. Nothing else leaves your wallet.

How long does a launch take?

Typically 20 to 40 seconds end to end, depending on congestion. Priority fees are included from the secured tier upward.

Can I test on devnet first?

Yes. Switch your wallet to devnet and Degenpad follows automatically. Devnet launches are free.

Your ticker is not going to mint itself.
